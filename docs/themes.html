Themes

Colors follow your wallpaper by default. A fixed theme keeps its own colors whatever the wallpaper is.

From the wallpaper

Matugen builds a palette from the current wallpaper each time it changes. The shell, window borders, kitty, GTK apps, folder icons, Vesktop and Zed all take it. Pick the Sweeteh theme in Zed to use it there.

Settings, Appearance has nine schemes that decide how the palette is drawn from the wallpaper, from Tonal spot, calm and muted, to Vibrant, the loudest. Or turn off Colors from wallpaper and choose one accent color.

Picking one

  • In Settings, Appearance, choose a name in the Theme row. Wallpaper goes back to wallpaper colors.
  • In the launcher, type >use the nord theme.
  • In the config, set theme.preset.
{
  "theme": {
    "preset": "catppuccin",
    "dark": true
  }
}

What a fixed theme changes Surfaces and text use the theme's exact colors, and the light under every key takes its accent. The scheme and accent options do nothing until you go back to Wallpaper.

Shape, type and motion

Settings, Appearance also holds roundness, spacing, the font and its size, animation speed, panel opacity and how much of the wallpaper's color seeps into the island. They are listed under theme in the config.
